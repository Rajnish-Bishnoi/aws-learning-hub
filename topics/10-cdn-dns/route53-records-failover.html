Route 53 (Part 2 – Record Sets & Failover)

AWS DNS📚 Study + Practical🎯 Interview Ready
📌 Concept🧠 Simple🏢 Company🗺️ Diagram🛠️ Practical⚡ Flow💻 Commands📋 Steps⚠️ Mistakes🎯 Questions📝 Notes🚀 Cheat Sheet🧠 Remember Trick

📌 Concept — What is it?

Route 53 record sets map names to targets and can support routing policies such as failover, where configured with health checks and suitable endpoints.

🧠 Simple Explanation — Yaad rehne wali language

Record = DNS answer; Failover = primary down ho to secondary answer.

🏢 Real Company Scenario

A service uses a primary endpoint and a secondary recovery endpoint with health-based DNS routing.

  • Skipping verification between steps.

🔐 Security / Best Practices

  • Restrict network exposure.
  • Keep credentials/private keys out of source code.
  • Monitor logs and important resource signals.
