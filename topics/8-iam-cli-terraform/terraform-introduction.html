📋 Step-by-Step Lab

  1. Understand the requirement and expected result.
  2. Open the relevant cloud console or CLI and choose a small lab configuration.
  3. Create/configure the resource with minimum required permissions and exposure.
  4. Verify dependencies before moving to the next step.
  5. Test the expected behavior and inspect logs/status/output.
  6. Record the command, result, error and fix in My Practical Notes.
  7. Stop/delete billable lab resources after the exercise.

⚠️ Common Mistakes

  • Wrong Region/resource/account/network path.
  • Skipping verification between steps.
  • Opening more access than needed.
  • Running destructive commands without confirming the target.
  • Leaving billable lab resources running.

🔐 Security / Best Practices

  • Use least privilege.
  • Restrict network exposure.
  • Keep credentials/private keys out of source code.
  • Monitor logs and important resource signals.
